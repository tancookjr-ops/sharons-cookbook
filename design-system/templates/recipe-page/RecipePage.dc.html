Roasted Squash & Sage Soup

Autumn in a bowl

Deeply roasted squash blended silky-smooth with fresh sage — a natural home for the garden harvest.

Soups & stews Canadian Lunch Easy vegan eoe-friendly garden

Prep
15 min
Cook
55 min
Total
70 min
Serves
6

Ingredients

6 servings
  • 1.2 kgbutternut squashpeeled and cubed✓
  • 1onion✓
  • 45 mlolive oil✓
  • 8fresh sageleaves, from the garden✓
  • 1 lvegetable stock·
  • 15 mlmaple syrup (optional)Nova Scotia's finest·

✓ = in your pantry

Instructions

  1. Roast

    Toss squash and onion with oil, spread on a tray and roast until caramelised at the edges.

    ⏱ 40 min🌡 200 °CSheet pan

  2. Crisp the sage

    Fry sage leaves in a little oil for 30 seconds until crisp; set a few aside for garnish.

  3. Blend

    Simmer the roasted vegetables in stock for 10 minutes, add most of the sage, then blend until completely smooth. Season, adding maple syrup if the squash needs help.

    ⏱ 10 minBlender
