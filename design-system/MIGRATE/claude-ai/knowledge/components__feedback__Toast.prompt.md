# Toast

A transient confirmation message — dark ink surface, cream text, coloured left border by tone. In the app these appear bottom-centre and fade after a couple of seconds. Keep copy short and past-tense.

```jsx
<Toast tone="success">Recipe saved</Toast>
<Toast tone="warn">A recipe needs a title</Toast>
<Toast tone="error">Could not read that image</Toast>
```

- **tone**: `success` (sage) | `warn` (amber) | `error` (terracotta)
